The Amnesia Problem: Why Your AI Agent Needs a Memory Upgrade

Imagine having to relearn everything every time you opened your favorite app. That's basically what happens with most AI agents – they have no memory, so every interaction starts from scratch. This means paying significant token costs re-injecting context, which can add up quickly.

The Problem: No Memory Means No Context

Without a decent memory system, AI agents can't:

  • Learn from past interactions
  • Keep track of user preferences or settings
  • Build knowledge over time – they're essentially stateless tools

Implementing effective memory systems is complex because you need to handle storage, retrieval, summarization, and context management. It's not just about storing data; it's about making sense of it.
