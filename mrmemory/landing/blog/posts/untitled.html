The Frustration of Stateless AI Agents

You've spent hours crafting the perfect response to a user's question, only for your stateless AI agent to promptly forget everything. It's like trying to have a conversation with a toddler - every interaction starts from scratch. Repeated questions, lost goals between steps, and a lack of personalization are just a few symptoms of this problem.

Memory: The Key to Intelligent Agents

For agents to be truly intelligent, they need memory. Not just conversation history, but the ability to store and retrieve context-specific information, guide multi-step plans with derived state, and persist facts and preferences across sessions.

Production agents typically require at least three memory functions:

  • Storing relevant data from each step
  • Fetching only the necessary items for the next step
  • Merging or decaying outdated information

Solving the Problem with MrMemory

MrMemory is a managed memory API that makes adding memory to your Python AI agent a breeze. With just 3 lines of code, you can give your agent a real memory:


from mrmemory import MrMemory
client = MrMemory(api_key="your-key")
client.remember("user prefers dark mode", tags=["preferences"])
