The Problem with Forgotten Context

When customer support agents can't recall a crucial procedure, it's not because they forgot a fact – it's because their memory management is broken. This leads to:

  • Repeated instructions causing increased latency
  • Zero personalization across sessions
  • Reduced accuracy in knowledge recall

We're not just talking about a minor inconvenience; we're talking about agent performance that's holding your business back.

Measuring Memory Performance with STATE-Bench

STATE-Bench, an open-source benchmark developed by Microsoft, puts AI agents to the test. It evaluates task outcomes, consistency across runs, operational cost, and interaction quality – all key metrics for a successful conversational agent.
